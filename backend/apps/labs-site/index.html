Yes. The request starts a preview workflow. You inspect the website and its supporting plan before deciding whether to activate the managed service.

No. The request does not publish a website, send a campaign or charge a card. Production launch happens only after approval and secure checkout.

How fast is the preview?

A preview can be ready in hours when the business information is complete and verifiable. Missing or conflicting facts are held for review rather than guessed.

No. Public business claims must come from supplied or verifiable evidence. When something is missing, we ask or leave it out.
