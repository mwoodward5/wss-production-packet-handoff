Good details make a better start.

A few practical notes can make the first conversation more useful.

The property location, the room or fixture involved, when you first noticed the issue, and any access details you already know are useful starting points.

Can I ask about a planned fixture change?

Yes. Share the fixture type, the room, and whether you already have a product in mind. The conversation can help identify the next planning step.
