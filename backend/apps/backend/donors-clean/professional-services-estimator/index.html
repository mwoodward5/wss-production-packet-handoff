Before you send plans

Straight answers on outsourced estimating

How is the fee calculated?

A fixed fee quoted after we see the plan set, based on scope and sheet count. You approve it before any work begins.

Whose pricing do you use?

Yours wherever you have it — your labour rates, crews and markup. We supply current regional material pricing for the rest.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, on request and before receiving documents. We never bid work ourselves, so there is no conflict of interest.

What if the plans are incomplete?

We flag the gaps as RFI items rather than assuming through them, and we will tell you when a set is not bidable.

Can you hit a shorter deadline?

Often. Twenty-four hour turnaround is available on a rush fee where our estimator capacity allows it that week.
