How a replacement runs

Five steps to a system that fits the house

  1. Home assessment

    We measure the envelope, count the windows and check the duct work before quoting anything.

  2. Load calculation

    A Manual J for your house, so the equipment matches actual heat loss instead of the old nameplate.

  3. Written options

    Two or three real options with efficiency, noise, rebate value and honest payback, on one page.

  4. Installation

    One or two days, drop cloths down, old equipment hauled, and the system commissioned before we leave.

  5. Commissioning report

    Static pressure, temperature rise and combustion numbers printed and handed to you with the warranty.

Before you replace

Straight answers on heating and cooling

How long does a replacement take?

Most furnace or heat pump swaps take one day. Adding duct work or converting fuel types can run to two.

Do heat pumps work here?

Cold-climate models hold capacity well below zero. We size them with a documented backup point rather than guessing.

What does a maintenance plan include?

Two visits a year, priority dispatch during cold snaps, discounted repairs and a written condition report each visit.

Will you match a lower quote?

We will show you what the other quote leaves out. If it is genuinely the same scope, we will talk price honestly.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through third-party lenders, and we will always show the cash price beside the financed one.
