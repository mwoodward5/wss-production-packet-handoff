How care works

Five steps of a treatment plan

  1. Consultation

    Forty-five minutes with a clinician: history, skin analysis, goals, and what treatment cannot achieve.

  2. Written plan

    A staged plan with costs and timing. You are welcome to leave and think about it — nothing is sold same-day.

  3. Treatment

    Performed by the clinician who assessed you, with dosing recorded in your notes.

  4. Two-week review

    Included with injectable treatment. Adjustments at review are part of the price, not an upsell.

  5. Maintenance

    Reviews at three to six months, with dosing adjusted down where results are holding.

Before you book

Straight answers on aesthetic treatment

Who performs the treatments?

The clinician who assessed you, under the supervision of our physician. You will not be handed to a different injector.

Will I look overdone?

Our dosing is deliberately conservative with a two-week review. It is easier to add than to wait out too much.

Is the consultation free?

The forty-five minute consultation carries a fee, which is credited against your first treatment if you proceed.

Are results guaranteed?

No honest clinic guarantees results. We quote realistic session counts and review outcomes in writing at each stage.

What if I change my mind about filler?

Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved. We will do it for our own work at no charge within the first month.
