Representation, in six movements

  • 01

    Buyer representation

    Search, shortlist and showings; offer strategy and terms; inspections and closing coordination — one advocate from first tour to keys.

  • 02

    Listing & marketing

    Prep planning, staging guidance, photography-ready presentation, showings and feedback reviews — a listing strategy set before the sign goes up.

  • 03

    Market valuation

    A comparable-sales valuation for your home or target property, with the assumptions written down — a number you can interrogate, not just receive.
