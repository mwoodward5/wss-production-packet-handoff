How a project runs

Five steps from consult to care plan

  1. Site consult

    We walk the property, test drainage and talk honestly about budget before drawing anything.

  2. Design

    Scaled plan, plant schedule and materials board, presented in person with two options where it matters.

  3. Estimate & schedule

    Phased pricing so you can build in stages without the design falling apart.

  4. Build & plant

    Hardscape first, soil amended properly, then planting at the right season for establishment.

  5. Care handover

    A written seasonal calendar, irrigation map and the option of our maintenance crew.

Before you plant

Straight answers on landscape work

When is the best time to plant?

Autumn and early spring in {{REGION}}. We will schedule installation for establishment rather than for our convenience.

Can we build in phases?

Yes. The design is drawn so hardscape, planting and lighting can be built across two or three seasons without rework.

Do you offer maintenance?

Yes, weekly through monthly visits, and we hand over a written calendar whether or not you use our crew.

What does the plant guarantee cover?

Replacement of failed plant material for three establishment years, provided the watering plan was followed.

Do you handle drainage problems?

Often it is the first thing we address. Grading and water movement come before planting on nearly every [[NEED:COUNTY]]{{COUNTY}}[[/NEED]][[NEED:!COUNTY]]area[[/NEED]] slope.
