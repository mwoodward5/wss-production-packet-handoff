What we do

Electrical services, quoted in writing

From a dead circuit on a Sunday morning to a full service upgrade — this is the work, and every job starts the same way: a look, then a written estimate.

  • Residential electrical

    Repairs, additions and remodel circuits for houses and condos — outlets, lighting, sub-panels and everything behind the walls.

  • Commercial electrical

    Tenant fit-outs, three-phase circuits, service gear and lighting for shops, offices and small industrial units.

  • Panel upgrades

    100 A to 200 A service panels, sub-panels, breaker replacement and service relocation — permitted and inspected.

  • EV charger installation

    Level 2 chargers on a dedicated 240-volt circuit, with a load check first so the service can actually carry it.

  • Generator installation

    Standby generators, transfer switches and inlet boxes — kept ready so the next outage is an inconvenience, not a weekend.

  • Lighting & fixtures

    Recessed, pendant and exterior lighting, dimmer circuits, LED retrofits and fixture swaps that don't void the listing.

  • Troubleshooting & repair

    Dead circuits, flicker, warm plates, breakers that trip at random — found with meters, fixed at the source, not by guessing.

  • Wiring & rewiring

    New circuits, service replacements and older-home rewiring — cloth wiring and ungrounded outlets brought to today's code.

  • General electrical

    Outlets, switches, smoke and CO alarms, exhaust fans, doorbells — the small jobs that keep a house working.

How it runs

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 1

    Describe the job

    Phone, email or the form — we ask the questions a real estimate needs: symptoms, ages, panel size, what changed recently.

  2. 2

    We come and look

    An on-site check: test, measure, open the panel if needed. Guesswork is not an estimate.

  3. 3

    Written estimate

    An itemized scope and price before any work begins. Nothing starts until you have it in hand.

  4. 4

    Done, tested, tidy

    The work, then the proof: circuits tested under load, labels on the panel, and the space left clean.

Do you give written estimates?+

Yes — every job above minor repairs gets a written, itemized estimate before work starts. Panel upgrades, EV chargers and generator hookups are always quoted in writing.

How do I know my panel needs an upgrade?+

Frequent breaker trips, flicker when large loads start, a fuse box or a panel under 100 amps, warm panel surfaces, or two-prong outlets everywhere are the usual signs. An on-site check settles it.

Can you install an EV charger at my home?+

Yes. Most homes need a dedicated 240-volt circuit; some need a panel upgrade first. We load-check the service, quote the circuit or upgrade, then install and test the charger you own or plan to buy.
