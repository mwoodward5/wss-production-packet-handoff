Before you reach out

Straight answers on fence work

How deep do you set posts?

Thirty-six inches in concrete for privacy and gate posts, deeper on sandy soil or long gate spans. Depth is written on the quote.

Do you handle HOA and permit paperwork?

Yes. We submit the drawing and material spec to your HOA or to {{CITY}} where a permit is required, and we do not start until it clears.

How long does a typical yard take?

Most residential lines take two working days: posts and concrete on day one, panels and gates on day two.

What does the warranty cover?

Fifteen years on post setting and workmanship, plus the manufacturer term on materials. It transfers with the property.

Can you match an existing fence?

Usually. Bring us a photo and a picket measurement and we will tell you honestly whether a match or a full run is the better spend.
