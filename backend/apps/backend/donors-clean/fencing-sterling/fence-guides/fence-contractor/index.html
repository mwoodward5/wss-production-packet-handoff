Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a fence company and a fence contractor?

In practice the terms describe the same business — one that installs and repairs fences. {{BUSINESS_NAME}} is both.

Do I need a contractor for a small repair?

Not always, but storm damage, leaning posts, or gate failures are usually footing or hardware problems, and those are worth an experienced eye before the next storm.

What questions should I ask a fence contractor?

Licensing and insurance proof, post depth and footing spec, hardware spec, the warranty terms in writing, a written itemized estimate, and nearby installs you can look at.

How are contractors priced?

By linear footage, height, material, gate count, terrain, and any custom features. Our estimates itemize each line.
