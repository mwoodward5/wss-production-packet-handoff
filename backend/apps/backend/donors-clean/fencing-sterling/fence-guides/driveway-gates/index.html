Frequently asked questions

How wide should a driveway gate be?

12–16 ft for single vehicle access; 16–20 ft for double-swing entries on wider drives.

Do you install gate openers?

Yes — swing and slide operators with keypad, remote, or smartphone control.

Will my driveway need electrical?

Automated gates need power at the gate location. We coordinate with a licensed electrician.

Can the gate open automatically when I arrive?

Yes — sensors, GPS, and smart openers can trigger on arrival.
