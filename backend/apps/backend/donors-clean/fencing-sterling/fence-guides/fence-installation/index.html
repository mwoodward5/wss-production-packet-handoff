Frequently asked questions

How long does fence installation take?

Most residential installs finish in 1–3 days once materials are on site.

How deep should fence posts be set?

We set posts roughly 1/3 the above-grade height plus the frost-line allowance, in concrete footings sized for the post.

Can you install on a slope?

Yes — we step or rack panels depending on grade and the look you want.

Do you install during winter?

Yes — we adjust concrete mix and scheduling. Severe freezes or saturated ground may push a job a few days.
