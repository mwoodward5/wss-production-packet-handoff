Frequently asked questions

How tall should a privacy fence be?

6 ft is standard for residential backyards; 8 ft is available where local rules allow.

Is wood or vinyl better for privacy?

Wood is more affordable up front and easier to repair. Vinyl is more expensive and essentially maintenance-free.

Will a privacy fence stop noise?

It reduces noise from street and neighbor activity but does not eliminate it. Solid board-on-board reduces more than dog-ear.

How long does a privacy fence last?

15–25 years for cedar and pressure-treated pine when maintained; longer for vinyl.
