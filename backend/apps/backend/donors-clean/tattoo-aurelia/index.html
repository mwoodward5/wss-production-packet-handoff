How booking works

Five steps from idea to healed tattoo

  1. Enquiry

    Send your idea, placement, size and reference images. We match you to the right artist, or tell you we are not it.

  2. Consultation

    Free, in person or by video. We talk placement, ageing, pain, budget and whether the idea fits the space.

  3. Design

    Your artist draws custom. You see it before the appointment and one revision round is included.

  4. Session

    Private room, single-use equipment, breaks whenever you need them, and no pressure to sit longer than you want.

  5. Aftercare

    Written aftercare, a check-in message at two weeks, and a free touch-up within six months.

Before you book

Straight answers on getting tattooed

Do you take walk-ins?

No. Every piece here is consulted and drawn in advance, which is why the studio stays appointment-only.

How much will it cost?

Most work is priced by the piece after the consultation, so you know the full number before booking. Deposits come off the final price.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits hold your artist's drawing time. They transfer once with 72 hours' notice and are non-refundable after that.

Can you tattoo over scars?

Often, once the scar is at least a year old and fully settled. We assess in person and never guarantee before seeing it.

What is your age policy?

Eighteen and over with government photo identification, no exceptions, in line with {{REGION}} law.
