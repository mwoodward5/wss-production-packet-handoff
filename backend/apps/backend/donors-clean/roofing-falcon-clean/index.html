How a roof runs

Five steps from inspection to warranty

  1. Inspection

    We walk the roof, photograph everything, and give you an honest opinion on repair versus replacement.

  2. Documentation

    Dated photos, measurements and a scope you can hand to an adjuster or compare against another bid.

  3. Materials & scheduling

    Colour selection, delivery staging and a firm date, weather permitting — with the weather rule explained up front.

  4. Installation

    Tear-off, decking repair by the sheet, underlayment, flashing and shingles, usually inside one to two days.

  5. Cleanup & warranty

    Magnet sweep of the yard, final inspection photos and the workmanship warranty in writing.

Before you claim

Straight answers on roofing

Repair or replace?

If the roof is under fifteen years old with isolated damage, we repair. We will show you the photos behind either recommendation.

Do you handle insurance claims?

We document the damage and meet the adjuster on site. You file the claim; we make sure the scope reflects what is actually up there.

How long does a roof take?

Most {{CITY}} homes are torn off and finished in one to two days, weather permitting.

What about my landscaping?

We tarp beds, move what can be moved, and run a magnet sweep of the whole property at the end.

What does the warranty cover?

Twenty-five years on workmanship plus the manufacturer term on materials, and it transfers once with the home.
