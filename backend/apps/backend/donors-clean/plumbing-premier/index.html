Before you book

Straight answers on whole-home water

What is in the water assessment?

Static and dynamic pressure, hardness, water heater temperature, shutoff condition and a photo map of the mechanical points.

Are leak-sensing valves worth it?

For anyone who travels, yes. They close the main on abnormal flow, which is the only thing that stops an unattended burst.

How disruptive is a repipe?

We stage it so water is restored every evening. Most homes take three to five days including finishing coordination.

Do you handle slab leaks?

Yes. We locate first, then advise reroute versus spot repair based on line age and access, with the reasoning in writing.

Is the warranty transferable?

The ten-year repipe warranty transfers once with the property, which helps at resale.
