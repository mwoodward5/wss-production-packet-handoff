WSS Connect

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

WSS Connect, operated by WSS Labs, is a unified inbox that consolidates a business's incoming messages — from text, email, phone/voicemail, website chat, and connected platforms — into one place, and lets that business reply. This policy explains what data WSS Connect handles, why, and how it's protected.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to businesses using WSS Connect (the "account holder") and, where relevant, to the customers or leads who message that business through a connected channel. WSS Labs acts as a service provider to the business account holder for the purpose of routing and displaying those messages — we do not sell message content or contact data to third parties.

2. What we collect

DataWhy
Message content (text, email, chat, call transcripts/voicemail, social DMs) Displayed to the business owner in their inbox so they can respond to leads and customers.
Contact info of the sender (phone number, email address, or platform profile) So the business owner knows who's messaging and can reply through the right channel.
OAuth access tokens for connected platforms (e.g. a Facebook Page or Instagram Business account) Used server-side only, to fetch and send messages on that platform. Tokens are never sent to or stored in the browser.
Account holder's PIN / access token Authenticates the business owner into their own inbox.

3. Connected platforms

When a business connects a platform such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, TikTok, or Google through WSS Connect, we request only the permissions needed to read and send messages on that platform on the business's behalf (for example, Meta's pages_messaging and instagram_manage_messages scopes). Each platform's own privacy policy also governs how it handles data — connecting through WSS Connect does not change your rights or obligations under that platform's policy.

4. How data is stored and protected

  • Message and connector data is stored in an access-controlled database (Supabase/PostgreSQL) with row-level security enabled.
  • All traffic between WSS Connect and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Platform access tokens are used only by our server-side functions and are never exposed to the browser or included in any client-side response.
  • We do not sell message content, contact information, or connector data to third parties.

5. Data retention

We retain messages and connector data for as long as the business account remains active with WSS Labs, so the inbox stays complete and useful. A business owner can request deletion of their account's message history and disconnection of any platform at any time by contacting us.

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect any connected platform at any time — this revokes WSS Connect's access token for that platform.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your business's message data by contacting us below.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as WSS Connect adds new connected channels or features. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
