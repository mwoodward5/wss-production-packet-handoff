No tech skills needed. If you can text a friend, you can do this. Ten minutes, start to finish.

1

Tell your crew about your business

Sign in and answer five simple questions: your business name, what you do, your hours, your service area, and how you like appointments booked. Type them like you'd tell a new employee on their first day.

💡 Don't overthink it. You can change any answer, any time, and your crew learns the update instantly.
2

Call your own crew — for practice

Tap the Practice Call button and your phone rings. That's your new team member answering. Ask them anything a customer would ask. It's free, private, and you can do it as many times as you want.

💡 Try being a difficult customer. Interrupt them. Ask about prices. See how they handle it — that's the fun part.
3

Flip the switch

When you love how they sound, forward your business line. Your phone shows the exact steps for your carrier — usually pressing about five buttons. From that second, every call gets answered, noted, booked, and texted back. 24 hours a day.

💡 You stay in control: unforward any time with one code, and every call summary lands in your inbox.
