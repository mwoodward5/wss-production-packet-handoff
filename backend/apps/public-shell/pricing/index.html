Questions

Can I try AnswerCrew before adding a payment plan?

Yes. Start with the public demo or create an owner workspace. You can inspect the call notes, safety flow, and checkout handoff before paying. Live calling stays locked until the account has consent controls and an active plan.

What happens when minutes run out?

Calling pauses at the included-minute limit. There are no automatic overage charges. You can add minutes, move up a plan, or wait for the monthly reset.

Can customers stop texts or calls?

Yes. Stop words are treated as suppression requests, and outbound paths are designed to fail closed when suppression status is unclear.
