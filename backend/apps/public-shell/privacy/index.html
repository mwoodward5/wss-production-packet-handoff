Privacy Policy

Plain-English launch privacy page for AnswerCrew. WSS Labs should have counsel review before broad paid traffic.

Information We Process

AnswerCrew may process account details, call metadata, transcripts or summaries, consent status, suppression requests, billing identifiers, and support messages needed to operate the service.

How We Use It

We use this information to answer and summarize calls, prepare follow-up, enforce consent and suppression rules, support accounts, prevent misuse, and maintain billing access.

Sharing

We use service providers such as hosting, database, voice, messaging, email, and payment platforms. We do not sell customer call data.

Stop Requests

STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, and QUIT requests are handled as suppression requests so future outreach can be blocked.
