007 / FAQ

Questions we hear most.

How long does new concrete take to cure before I can use it?

Foot traffic in 24-48 hours. Vehicles in 7 days for residential drives. Full design strength at 28 days.

What thickness do I need for my slab?

Sidewalks and patios are typically 4 inches. Residential drives 4-5 inches with reinforcement. Commercial slabs 6 inches and up, designed around the loads.

Stamped concrete vs pavers - which is better?

Stamped concrete is one continuous slab — nothing to shift or weed. For most residential patios and pool decks, stamped wins on price and long-term appearance.

How far do you travel?

50-mile radius from Sparta, TN. Larger commercial work outside that radius is case-by-case.

Do you handle the haul-off?

Yes. Demolition pricing includes loading, hauling and disposal so the site is left clean.
